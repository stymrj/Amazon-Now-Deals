FAQ

Good questions.

Is it safe? Does it see my data?

It runs inside your browser tab using your own Amazon Now session. There is no server and no analytics. Saved items and price history stay in your browser.

Why do I get no results at first?

Amazon Now needs your location and brand ID. Open amazon.in/tez/browse/home first, then scan again. You can also paste the Brand ID in the sidebar.

Is it official?

No. It is an independent tool, not affiliated with Amazon Now. It only reads what your account can already see.

Will it keep working?

Amazon Now can change its internal APIs at any time. The parser reads responses loosely to survive small changes, and the tool tells you when a scan returns nothing.

How does price-drop tracking work?

After each scan the tool remembers every price in your browser. Next time, new products are labelled New and cheaper ones Price drop.

Where does the data come from?

Live from Amazon Now, inside your own logged-in tab and for your delivery address. Nothing is stored on any server; saved items and price history stay in your browser.
